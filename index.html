How accurate is this?
  • Outdoors with open sky, iPhone GPS is usually within about 5–10 m (15–30 ft).
  • Indoors it often drifts 20–50 m+ (65–165 ft). Treat interior pins as notes on a sketch, not measured rooms.
  • The live map shows a blue circle for your phone's reported accuracy. The trail skips very rough readings and impossible jumps, and smooths out small jitter.
  • Pins average your last few seconds of readings. Stand still for 3–5 seconds before pinning for the best result.
  • iPhone pauses location when the screen locks or Safari goes to the background. Keep the app open while you walk.
